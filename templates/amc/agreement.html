1

Background

1.1Ralfiz has developed and/or deployed the software, application(s), website(s) and/or related systems described in Schedule A (the “Covered System”) for the Client.

1.2The Client wishes to engage Ralfiz to provide ongoing maintenance and technical support for the Covered System, and Ralfiz agrees to provide such services on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2

Definitions

  • “Covered System” – the software, mobile app(s), website(s), web application(s), APIs, admin dashboards and servers listed in Schedule A.
  • “Bug / Defect” – any failure of the Covered System to function as per its approved specification at the time of handover.
  • “Enhancement” – any new feature, module, screen, report, workflow or change in business logic not part of the original approved scope.
  • “Support Hours” – Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, excluding public holidays in Kerala.
  • “Response Time” – time between the Client logging an issue through an approved channel and Ralfiz acknowledging it and starting work.
  • “Resolution Time” – time taken to fix the issue or provide an acceptable workaround.
  • “Third-Party Services” – services not owned by Ralfiz, including hosting, cloud servers, domains, SSL, SMS/WhatsApp/email gateways, payment gateways, maps, app stores, Firebase and similar.
3

Term

3.1This Agreement shall be valid for {{ months_words }} ({{ months }}) months from {{ amc.start_date|date:"d/m/Y" }} to {{ amc.end_date|date:"d/m/Y" }} (the “AMC Period”).

3.2The Agreement may be renewed for further periods of twelve (12) months by mutual written consent and payment of the applicable renewal fee. Ralfiz will send a renewal notice at least 30 days before expiry.

3.3Renewal fees may be revised by up to 10% annually, or as mutually agreed, to reflect changes in scope, technology or infrastructure costs.

4

Scope of Services

Ralfiz shall provide the following services for the Covered System during the AMC Period:

4.1 Corrective Maintenance

  • Identification and fixing of bugs and defects in the Covered System.
  • Fixing issues caused by errors in code delivered by Ralfiz.

4.2 Preventive Maintenance

  • Periodic health checks of application and server performance.
  • Monitoring of logs, errors and uptime (where monitoring access is available).
  • Database optimisation and clean-up as required.

4.3 Adaptive Maintenance

  • Updates required due to changes in OS versions (Android / iOS), browser updates, or mandatory third-party API changes, limited to keeping existing features working.
  • Updating framework and package dependencies for security and compatibility.
  • Resubmission of mobile app builds to Google Play Store / Apple App Store when required for compliance (client developer account required).

4.4 Security & Backup

  • Application of security patches and SSL renewal support.
  • Periodic database backups as per Schedule B (where hosting is managed by Ralfiz).
  • Restoration support from available backups in case of data loss.

4.5 Technical Support

  • Support via {{ amc.plan_terms.channels }} during Support Hours.
  • Remote assistance for usage-related queries.
  • Minor content updates (text, images, banners) within the allowance in clause 4.6.

4.6 Minor Changes Allowance

  • Small modifications (e.g., label changes, minor UI tweaks, simple report field additions) up to {{ amc.plan_terms.hours }} hours per month. Unused hours do not carry forward.
5

Exclusions

The following are not covered under this AMC and shall be quoted and billed separately:

  1. New features, modules, integrations, redesigns or Enhancements.
  2. Issues caused by changes, modifications or access to the code/server by the Client or any third party.
  3. Issues arising from Third-Party Services, including outages, pricing changes, policy changes or discontinuation.
  4. Costs of hosting, servers, domains, SSL certificates, SMS/WhatsApp/email credits, payment gateway fees, app store developer accounts, paid APIs or licenses.
  5. Data entry, data migration, bulk data correction or data recovery beyond available backups.
  6. Hardware, networking, printers, POS devices, or client-side device/OS issues.
  7. Damage due to virus attacks, hacking or misuse resulting from the Client’s negligence (e.g., shared passwords, compromised admin accounts).
  8. On-site visits, unless specified in Schedule B (on-site visits outside Perinthalmanna are chargeable with travel costs).
  9. Training for new staff beyond {{ amc.plan_terms.training }} session{{ amc.plan_terms.training|pluralize }} per year.
  10. Major version upgrades (e.g., framework migration, full platform rebuild).

7

Client Responsibilities

The Client shall:

  1. Report issues only through the approved support channels, with clear details, screenshots and steps to reproduce.
  2. Nominate a single point of contact (SPOC) for coordination.
  3. Provide necessary access (server, hosting panel, app store accounts, third-party dashboards) in a timely manner.
  4. Ensure timely renewal and payment of Third-Party Services (hosting, domain, SSL, gateways, etc.).
  5. Not modify the source code or server configuration without informing Ralfiz in writing.
  6. Maintain secure credentials and restrict admin access to authorised personnel.
  7. Test and approve fixes within 5 working days; fixes are deemed accepted if no feedback is received within this period.

9

Intellectual Property

9.1Ownership of the Covered System remains as defined in the original development agreement between the Parties.

9.2Any bug fixes or minor updates made under this AMC shall form part of the Covered System and follow the same ownership terms.

9.3Ralfiz retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, reusable components and know-how used in providing the services.

10

Confidentiality & Data Protection

10.1Each Party shall keep confidential all business, technical and customer information of the other Party received under this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

10.2Ralfiz shall access Client data only to the extent necessary to perform the services and shall take reasonable measures to protect it.

10.3The Client remains the owner and data controller of all data stored in the Covered System and is responsible for compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, in relation to its users’ data.

10.4This clause survives termination of the Agreement for a period of 2 years.

11

Termination

11.1Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days’ written notice.

11.2Either Party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 15 days of notice.

11.3On termination by the Client without cause, no refund shall be due for the remaining period. On termination by Ralfiz without cause, Ralfiz shall refund the unused portion of the AMC fee on a pro-rata basis.

11.4On termination, Ralfiz shall hand over any Client credentials in its possession and provide a reasonable handover of the latest code (if in Ralfiz’s custody and ownership belongs to the Client), subject to clearance of all outstanding dues.

12

Limitation of Liability

12.1Ralfiz shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, including loss of profit, revenue, business or data.

12.2Ralfiz’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the AMC fees paid by the Client for the current AMC Period.

12.3Ralfiz does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation, particularly where the system depends on Third-Party Services.

13

Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance due to events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, floods, pandemics, government actions, internet or power outages, cyber attacks on third-party infrastructure, or failure of Third-Party Services.

14

Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for 12 months thereafter, the Client shall not directly or indirectly hire or engage any employee of Ralfiz who worked on the Covered System, without Ralfiz’s prior written consent.

15

Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

15.1This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

15.2The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute amicably through mutual discussion within 30 days.

15.3Unresolved disputes shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Perinthalmanna, Kerala, and the language shall be English.

15.4Subject to the above, the courts at Perinthalmanna, Kerala shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

16

General

16.1Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement regarding the AMC and supersedes prior discussions on the same subject.

16.2Amendments: Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

16.3Assignment: The Client may not assign this Agreement without Ralfiz’s written consent.

16.4Independent Contractor: Ralfiz acts as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, employment or agency relationship.

16.5Notices: Notices shall be sent to the addresses/emails mentioned above. Email communication shall be considered valid written notice.

16.6Severability: If any clause is held invalid, the remaining clauses shall continue in full force.
