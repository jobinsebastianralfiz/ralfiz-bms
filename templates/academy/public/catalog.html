Questions

Is this a Microsoft certification?

No. Ralfiz Academy prepares you for the Microsoft exams, which you book and take with Microsoft. When you finish a course you receive a Ralfiz Academy certificate of completion.

What do I need for the labs?

Most labs run in a free Microsoft developer environment; the first lesson of each course shows you how to set it up. PL-300 labs use Power BI Desktop, which runs on Windows.

How is my progress tracked?

You get your own student login. Lab steps, quiz answers and practice test scores are saved to your account, so you can continue on any device. Courses unlock one after another as you complete them.

Which course should I start with?

Start with PL-900 Power Platform Fundamentals. Then take AB-410 to build apps or PL-300 to analyse data. AB-400 is for developers and comes after AB-410.
