Privacy Policy

Ralfiz Digital - Employee Portal

1. Introduction

Ralfiz Digital ("we", "our", "us") operates the Ralfiz Digital Employee Portal mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Full name, employee ID, and designation
  • Email address and phone number
  • Department and employment details
  • Address and emergency contact information
  • Date of birth and joining date
  • Profile photo

2.2 Biometric Data

  • Facial photograph for attendance verification
  • Facial recognition encoding data (mathematical representation used solely for identity verification)

2.3 Location Data

  • GPS coordinates at the time of attendance check-in and check-out
  • Location data is used exclusively to verify you are within the designated office area

2.4 Device Information

  • Device token for push notifications
  • Device platform (Android/iOS)

2.5 Usage Data

  • Attendance records (check-in/check-out times, verification method)
  • Leave requests and approvals
  • Work assignments and status updates

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Employee Management: Managing your employment profile and records
  • Attendance Tracking: Verifying and recording your daily attendance using face recognition, QR codes, and/or location verification
  • Leave Management: Processing and tracking leave requests
  • Work Assignments: Managing and tracking assigned tasks
  • Notifications: Sending you relevant updates about attendance, leave, and work assignments
  • Security: Preventing fraudulent attendance entries through biometric and location verification

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared with:

  • Your Employer (Ralfiz Digital): Management and HR personnel who need access to employee records for legitimate business purposes
  • Service Providers: Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for push notifications
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication tokens (JWT) are used for secure API access
  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing algorithms
  • Face encoding data is stored as mathematical vectors, not as reconstructable images
  • Access to employee data is restricted to authorized personnel only

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your personal data stored in the App
  • Correction: Update your personal information through the App or by contacting HR
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data through the App's "Delete Account" feature or by contacting us
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a structured format
  • Withdraw Consent: You may stop using the App at any time

7. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time through the App. Upon deletion:

  • Your employee profile and personal information will be permanently removed
  • All attendance records, leave requests, and work assignments will be deleted
  • Your face photo and biometric encoding will be permanently destroyed
  • Your user account credentials will be removed
  • Push notification tokens will be deactivated and deleted

This action is irreversible. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by employees of Ralfiz Digital only. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
