# WhatsApp messages — Internship Continuation Agreement

Ready-to-send drafts for the agreement signing links generated at
`/hr/agreements/send/`. Replace `[Name]` and `[link]` before sending, or use the
**WhatsApp** button on the send-results page, which fills both in for you.

WhatsApp formatting: `*text*` renders **bold**, `_text_` renders _italic_.

---

## 1. Intern paying a monthly fee

State the amount before they open the link. Someone who is told upfront makes a
decision; someone who discovers a charge inside the document feels sprung on.

```
Hi [Name],

We're continuing our internship programme and have put the terms in writing.

One thing to know before you read it: continued participation carries a fee of
*₹750 per month*.

It covers the structured side of the internship — mentorship on your assigned
work, technical guidance to build practical skills, learning materials and
resources for your domain, and support while you complete tasks.

Please read the full terms and tell us whether you'd like to continue:
[link]

Takes 2 minutes — read through and tap YES or NO at the end. The link is
personal to you, so please don't forward it.

Continuing is your choice. If you'd rather not, choose NO and that's completely
fine. Any questions, ask us before you sign.

— Ralfiz Technologies
```

> Change the amount if you set a different fee for that person on the send
> screen. The figure in the message must match the one in their agreement.

## 2. Intern on a free internship

Their agreement carries no fee section at all, so the message must not mention
one either.

```
Hi [Name],

We're continuing our internship programme and have put the terms in writing —
schedule, attendance, work and learning responsibilities, confidentiality, and
what continuation depends on. Your internship carries no monthly fee.

Please read and tell us whether you'd like to continue:
[link]

Takes 2 minutes. The link is personal to you.

— Ralfiz Technologies
```

## 4. Reminder, for an unopened or unanswered link

The dashboard at `/hr/agreements/` shows who has not responded, and marks the
ones who opened the link without answering.

```
Hi [Name],

Just a reminder to read the internship agreement and let us know your decision:
[link]

It expires on [date]. If you have any questions before deciding, message us.

— Ralfiz Technologies
```

## Notes on wording

**Avoid calling it a "policy change".** For most interns this is the first time
the terms have been written down at all, not a change to something they already
agreed to. "Change" invites _"what changed?"_ — and for anyone paying, that
question lands straight on the money.

**Keep "continuing is your choice."** A fee lands better when the alternative is
stated plainly, and the agreement already offers Discontinue as a real option.
The message should match the document.

**One message per person, never a group broadcast.** Each link is personal and
one-time — whoever opens it can sign as that intern. A group send also leaks who
is paying what.

**Answer questions before they sign, not after.** A signature collected from
someone who did not understand the fee is worth less than the two minutes it
takes to explain it.

---

## Open gap in the agreement itself

The document makes _"timely payment of the applicable monthly fee"_ a condition
of continuing (section 9) but never says **how or when to pay** — no due date, no
UPI or account details, no what-happens-if-late. Expect that question as soon as
people start saying yes.

Worth adding to the template before the next batch goes out. It is editable at
`/admin/employees/agreementtemplate/` with no deploy needed.
