# Practice set 10.2: Professional, technical, skill, value and environmental education

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. Each question has four options; choose one.

**1.** The regulator for vocational education and training in India is:
(1) NSDC  (2) NCVET  (3) AICTE  (4) NBA

**2.** Match List I with List II.

| List I (Field) | List II (Regulator) |
|---|---|
| A. Law | I. NCTE |
| B. Teacher education | II. Council of Architecture |
| C. Architecture | III. Bar Council of India |
| D. Engineering | IV. AICTE |

(1) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV  (2) A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV  (3) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**3.** A learner completes 900 notional learning hours. Under NCrF (30 hours = 1 credit), the credits earned are:
(1) 25  (2) 30  (3) 36  (4) 45

**4.** Arrange in chronological order: A. Tbilisi Conference, B. Stockholm Conference, C. Belgrade Charter, D. Rio Earth Summit.
(1) B, A, C, D  (2) C, B, A, D  (3) B, C, A, D  (4) B, C, D, A

**5.** Which is NOT one of the five Tbilisi categories of EE objectives?
(1) Awareness  (2) Skills  (3) Evaluation ability  (4) Participation

**6.** Statement I: Skill India was launched on 15 July 2015. Statement II: NCVET subsumed NSDA and NCVT.
(1) Both true  (2) Both false  (3) I true, II false  (4) I false, II true

**7.** NEP 2020’s target for exposure to vocational education is:
(1) 25% of learners by 2025  (2) 50% of learners by 2025  (3) 50% of learners by 2035  (4) 100% of learners by 2030

**8.** A student under the UGC four-year UG framework has 124 credits and leaves. The highest award she qualifies for is:
(1) UG certificate  (2) UG diploma  (3) Three-year UG degree  (4) Honours degree

**9.** Assertion (A): NBA and NAAC both accredit, but at different units. Reason (R): NBA accredits programmes while NAAC accredits institutions.
(1) Both true and R explains A  (2) Both true, R does not explain A  (3) A true, R false  (4) A false, R true

**10.** Arrange in chronological order: A. NCVET, B. NSQF, C. NSDC, D. National Credit Framework.
(1) C, B, A, D  (2) B, C, A, D  (3) C, A, B, D  (4) C, B, D, A

(1) T. M. A. Pai (2002)  (2) M. C. Mehta v. Union of India (1991)  (3) Unni Krishnan (1993)  (4) Vishaka (1997)

**13.** A 3-credit theory course (15 lecture hours per credit per semester) needs how many lecture hours in a semester?
(1) 30  (2) 40  (3) 45  (4) 60
