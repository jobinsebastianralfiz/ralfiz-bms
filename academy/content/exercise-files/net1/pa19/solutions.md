**8. Answer: (2)**
Universal access to mobile connectivity, IT for jobs, electronics manufacturing and early harvest programmes are all pillars. Make in India is a separate initiative (electronics manufacturing is the related Digital India pillar).

**9. Answer: (1)**
Because a worm replicates itself and needs no host file (R), it can spread without a user opening anything (A). R explains A.

**10. Answer: (3)**
Individuals filing returns online is a government-to-citizen service. G2E would be services to government employees as employees, such as salary or service records.

**11. Answer: (1)**
Encryption and access control keep data secret (confidentiality); hashes and signatures detect changes (integrity); backups and DoS protection keep systems usable (availability); passwords, OTPs and biometrics verify identity (authentication).

**12. Answer: (2)**
A message that impersonates a trusted sender to steal credentials, using a look-alike link, is phishing.

**13. Answer: (1)**
