# Practice set 9.1: Development, environment and human interaction

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** How many Sustainable Development Goals and targets does the 2030 Agenda contain?
(1) 8 goals, 21 targets  (2) 17 goals, 169 targets  (3) 17 goals, 231 targets  (4) 169 goals, 17 targets

**2.** Match List I (SDG number) with List II (goal).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. SDG 3 | I. Sustainable cities and communities |
| B. SDG 7 | II. Good health and well-being |
| C. SDG 11 | III. Responsible consumption and production |
| D. SDG 12 | IV. Affordable and clean energy |

(1) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I  (2) A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III  (3) A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III  (4) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III

**3.** Arrange in chronological order (earliest first).
A. Rio+20 conference  B. Publication of The Limits to Growth  C. Millennium Summit  D. Brundtland Report  E. World Summit on Sustainable Development
(1) B, D, C, E, A  (2) D, B, C, E, A  (3) B, D, E, C, A  (4) B, C, D, E, A

**4.** Statement I: The 2030 Agenda was adopted in September 2015. Statement II: The Millennium Development Goals had a deadline of 2015.
(1) Both statements are true  (2) Both statements are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**5.** Using I = P × A × T with P = 50 million people, A = US$ 8,000 per person and T = 0.25 kg CO₂ per US$, the annual impact is:
(1) 1 million tonnes  (2) 10 million tonnes  (3) 100 million tonnes  (4) 1 billion tonnes

**6.** Which of the following are regional-level environmental issues?
A. Acid rain across neighbouring countries  B. Pollution of the Ganga basin  C. Stratospheric ozone depletion  D. Smoke from crop burning over several states  E. A noisy workshop in one lane
(1) A, B and D only  (2) A, B, C and D only  (3) B, D and E only  (4) A and C only

**7.** The HDI of a country whose dimension indices are 0.9 (health), 0.5 (education) and 0.6 (income) is closest to:
(1) 0.667  (2) 0.270  (3) 0.646  (4) 0.600

**8.** Assertion (A): Groundwater tables have fallen sharply in parts of Punjab and Haryana. Reason (R): Free or subsidised electricity for tube wells encouraged heavy pumping for water-intensive crops such as paddy.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is true but (R) is false  (4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**9.** Which of the following is NOT one of the “five Ps” of the 2030 Agenda?
(1) Planet  (2) Peace  (3) Population  (4) Prosperity

**10.** Match List I (idea) with List II (associated thinker).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Environmental determinism | I. Vidal de la Blache |
| B. Possibilism | II. Griffith Taylor |
| C. Neo-determinism | III. Friedrich Ratzel |
| D. Tragedy of the commons | IV. Garrett Hardin |

(1) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV  (2) A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV  (3) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**11.** A city of 20 lakh people grows at 3.5% per year. Using the rule of 70, it will have about 40 lakh people after roughly:
(1) 10 years  (2) 20 years  (3) 35 years  (4) 70 years

**12.** Statement I: Environmental impacts of mining include acid mine drainage and loss of topsoil. Statement II: The urban heat island makes city centres cooler than surrounding rural areas at night.
(1) Both statements are true  (2) Both statements are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**13.** Which of the following SDGs deal directly with the natural environment?
A. SDG 13  B. SDG 14  C. SDG 15  D. SDG 5  E. SDG 6
(1) A, B and C only  (2) A, B, C and E only  (3) A, C, D and E only  (4) B, C and D only

**14.** A country’s footprint is 3.0 gha per person and its biocapacity 1.2 gha per person. How many times its own biocapacity does it use?
(1) 0.4  (2) 1.8  (3) 2.5  (4) 3.6

**15.** Arrange the following SDGs in descending order of goal number.
A. No poverty  B. Peace, justice and strong institutions  C. Decent work and economic growth  D. Reduced inequalities  E. Life on land
(1) B, E, D, C, A  (2) E, B, D, C, A  (3) B, D, E, C, A  (4) B, E, C, D, A
