# Practice set 7.2: Data interpretation

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 12 minutes, problems 9–15 in 12 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

## Set 1 (problems 1–5)

Production of food grains in four states (thousand tonnes)

| State | Rice | Wheat | Pulses |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 120 | 80 | 20 |
| S2 | 90 | 150 | 30 |
| S3 | 60 | 40 | 25 |
| S4 | 150 | 30 | 15 |

**1.** The total food-grain production of S2 is:
(1) 240 thousand tonnes  (2) 270 thousand tonnes  (3) 300 thousand tonnes  (4) 250 thousand tonnes

**2.** S2’s wheat production is what percentage of the total wheat production of the four states?
(1) 50%  (2) 55.6%  (3) 45%  (4) 60%

**3.** The ratio of rice production of S4 to that of S3 is:
(1) 2 : 5  (2) 3 : 1  (3) 5 : 2  (4) 5 : 3

**4.** Pulses form what percentage of S3’s total production?
(1) 25%  (2) 20%  (3) 18%  (4) 22.5%

**5.** The average rice production per state is:
(1) 100 thousand tonnes  (2) 110 thousand tonnes  (3) 105 thousand tonnes  (4) 115 thousand tonnes

## Set 2 (problems 6–10)

Copies sold by a publisher in 2024, by category (pie chart shown as percentages). Total copies sold = 3,60,000.

| Category | Share (%) |
|---|---|
| Textbooks | 35 |
| Fiction | 20 |
| Children’s books | 15 |
| Exam guides | 25 |
| Others | 5 |

**6.** The central angle of the Exam guides sector is:
(1) 25°  (2) 75°  (3) 90°  (4) 100°

**7.** The number of fiction copies sold is:
(1) 72,000  (2) 7,200  (3) 60,000  (4) 80,000

**8.** In 2025, fiction sales rise by 25% and all other categories sell the same number of copies as in 2024. Fiction’s share of total sales in 2025 is closest to:
(1) 25.0%  (2) 23.8%  (3) 22.5%  (4) 20.0%

**9.** Textbook sales exceed children’s book sales by:
(1) 20,000 copies  (2) 54,000 copies  (3) 72,000 copies  (4) 1,26,000 copies

**10.** The ratio of children’s books to Others is:
(1) 1 : 3  (2) 3 : 1  (3) 5 : 1  (4) 3 : 2

## Set 3 (problems 11–15)

Visitors to a heritage site, 2019–2024 (lakh), read from a line graph

| Year | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visitors | 40 | 12 | 18 | 30 | 45 | 54 |

**11.** The percentage fall in visitors from 2019 to 2020 was:
(1) 28%  (2) 233%  (3) 30%  (4) 70%

**12.** The percentage increase from 2023 to 2024 was:
(1) 20%  (2) 9%  (3) 16.7%  (4) 25%

**13.** In which year did the number of visitors first exceed the 2019 level?
(1) 2022  (2) 2023  (3) 2024  (4) It never did

**14.** The average number of visitors per year over 2019–2024 is closest to:
(1) 30.0 lakh  (2) 33.2 lakh  (3) 35.0 lakh  (4) 39.8 lakh

**15.** Visitors in 2024 were how many times the visitors in 2020, and what percentage increase is that?
(1) 4.5 times, a 450% increase  (2) 3.5 times, a 350% increase  (3) 4.5 times, a 350% increase  (4) 4 times, a 300% increase
