# Practice set 4.1: Communication: meaning, types and barriers

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 9 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** The stage of the communication process in which the sender converts an idea into words, symbols or gestures is called:
(1) decoding  (2) encoding  (3) feedback  (4) channelling

**2.** Match List I (code) with List II (concerned with).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Oculesics | I. Vocal cues such as tone, pitch and pauses |
| B. Olfactics | II. Clothing, jewellery and objects |
| C. Artifactics | III. Smell |
| D. Paralanguage | IV. Eye contact and gaze |

(1) A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I  (2) A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I  (3) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II  (4) A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I

**3.** Assertion (A): Noise can occur at any stage of the communication process.
Reason (R): Noise includes any interference, physical, physiological, semantic or psychological, that distorts the message.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true but (R) is false
(4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**4.** Statement I: Horizontal communication takes place between people at the same level of an organisation.
Statement II: Downward communication mainly carries feedback and suggestions from subordinates.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
(2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
(3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
(4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**5.** A research team of 10 members uses an all-channel network. How many two-way channels does it have?
(1) 90  (2) 45  (3) 10  (4) 9

**6.** Which of the following are barriers to inter-cultural communication?
A. Ethnocentrism
B. Stereotyping
C. Different norms of eye contact
D. Seeking feedback to check understanding
E. Language differences
(1) A, B and E only  (2) A, B, C and D only  (3) A, B, C and E only  (4) B, C, D and E only

**7.** Arrange Tuckman's original four stages of group development in order.
A. Performing  B. Forming  C. Norming  D. Storming
(1) B, C, D, A  (2) B, D, C, A  (3) D, B, C, A  (4) B, D, A, C

**8.** In Mehrabian's experiments on inconsistent messages about feelings, the share attributed to tone of voice was:
(1) 7%  (2) 55%  (3) 38%  (4) 93%

**9.** A classroom observation using Flanders' categories gives 84 tallies of teacher talk (categories 1–7), 30 of student talk (8–9) and 6 of silence or confusion (10). What percentage of the tallies is teacher talk?
(1) 84%  (2) 74%  (3) 64%  (4) 70%

**10.** In the Johari window, feedback from others mainly reduces which area?
(1) Open  (2) Hidden  (3) Blind  (4) Unknown

**11.** Which of the following is a feature of low-context communication?
(1) Meaning is carried mostly by relationships and shared history
(2) Messages are explicit, direct and carried mainly by words
(3) Silence and indirect hints carry most of the meaning
(4) Written contracts are regarded as unimportant

**12.** Which communication network is the most centralised and usually the fastest for simple tasks?
(1) Circle  (2) All-channel  (3) Chain  (4) Wheel

**13.** Assertion (A): The grapevine often spreads information faster than formal channels.
Reason (R): The grapevine does not have to follow the formal chain of command.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true but (R) is false

**14.** A department head passes on only the good news from his team and leaves out the problems when reporting to the Dean. This is an example of:
(1) semantic barrier  (2) physiological barrier  (3) filtering, an organisational barrier  (4) physical barrier

**15.** Which of the following best describes effective classroom communication?
(1) The teacher speaks continuously so that no time is lost.
(2) The teacher uses questions, examples and students' responses to adjust the explanation.
(3) Students take notes silently and ask nothing until the examination.
(4) All explanations are given only in writing.
