# Practice set 2.1: Research: meaning, types and methods

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 9 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** A district education officer commissions a study to find why dropout is high in tribal hostels and what can be done about it in the coming year. The study is best classified as:
(1) Fundamental research  (2) Applied research  (3) Historical research  (4) Conceptual research

**2.** Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of scientific research?
(1) It is empirical  (2) It is replicable  (3) Its findings are final and beyond revision  (4) It is systematic

**3.** Match List I (thinker) with List II (idea).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Auguste Comte | I. Falsifiability |
| B. Karl Popper | II. Grounded theory |
| C. Thomas Kuhn | III. Law of three stages |
| D. Glaser and Strauss | IV. Paradigm shift |

(1) A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II  (2) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II  (3) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**4.** A researcher compares the mathematics scores of students who studied in English-medium and regional-medium schools, using their existing records. The method is:
(1) True experimental  (2) Quasi-experimental  (3) Ex post facto  (4) Action research

**5.** Assertion (A): Post-positivists use triangulation.
Reason (R): Post-positivists believe that every single method is imperfect and open to bias.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is true but (R) is false  (4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**6.** Checking whether a palm-leaf manuscript really dates from the century it claims is:
(1) Internal criticism  (2) External criticism  (3) Content validity  (4) Member checking

**7.** Statement I: A trend study draws different samples from the same population at different times.
Statement II: A cohort study follows a group defined by a shared experience, such as the year of birth.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true  (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**8.** Which of the following are true experimental designs? A. Posttest-only control group design (random assignment) B. One-group pretest–posttest design C. Solomon four-group design D. Static-group comparison E. Pretest–posttest control group design (random assignment)
(1) A, B and C only  (2) C, D and E only  (3) A, C and E only  (4) A, C, D and E only

**9.** The verification principle is associated with:
(1) Critical realism  (2) Logical positivism (the Vienna Circle)  (3) Phenomenology  (4) Pragmatism

**10.** Arrange the stages of an action research cycle in the correct order: A. Reflect B. Plan C. Observe D. Act
(1) B, C, D, A  (2) B, D, A, C  (3) D, B, C, A  (4) B, D, C, A

**11.** Statement I: A very high correlation between two variables proves that one causes the other.
Statement II: Correlational research is a type of descriptive research.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true  (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**12.** Match List I (qualitative tradition) with List II (focus).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Phenomenology | I. Culture of a group through immersion |
| B. Ethnography | II. Theory emerging from data |
| C. Grounded theory | III. Lived experience of a phenomenon |
| D. Narrative research | IV. Life stories told by individuals |

(1) A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV  (2) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV  (3) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**13.** In a mixed-methods study, focus groups are held first to discover how students talk about exam stress, and a questionnaire built from those themes is then given to 1,200 students. The design is:
(1) Convergent parallel  (2) Explanatory sequential  (3) Exploratory sequential  (4) Solomon four-group

**14.** Which of the following are associated with interpretivism? A. Verstehen B. Multiple, socially constructed realities C. Value-free, detached observer D. Idiographic understanding E. Universal laws of social behaviour
(1) A, B, C and D only  (2) B, D and E only  (3) A and E only  (4) A, B and D only

**15.** Assertion (A): A true experiment is the strongest design for establishing cause and effect.
Reason (R): A true experiment combines manipulation of the independent variable, control of other variables and random assignment.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is true but (R) is false  (4) (A) is false but (R) is true
