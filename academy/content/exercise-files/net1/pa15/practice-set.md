# Practice set 6.3: Indian logic

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 12 minutes, problems 9–15 in 12 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** According to Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā, the knowledge “there is no book on the table” (in good light) is obtained through:
(1) Pratyakṣa  (2) Anumāna  (3) Anupalabdhi  (4) Arthāpatti

**2.** Match List I (school) with List II (pramāṇas accepted).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Nyāya | I. Perception, inference, testimony |
| B. Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā | II. Perception, inference, comparison, testimony |
| C. Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā | III. Perception, inference, comparison, testimony, postulation |
| D. Sāṅkhya | IV. All six pramāṇas |

(1) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I  (2) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I  (3) A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I  (4) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV

**3.** Arrange the members of the inference for others in the correct order.
A. Conclusion  B. Reason  C. Application  D. Example  E. Thesis
(1) E, B, D, C, A  (2) E, D, B, C, A  (3) B, E, D, C, A  (4) E, B, C, D, A

**4.** “The lake has fire, because it has smoke.” The fallacy is:
(1) Viruddha  (2) Svarūpāsiddha  (3) Bādhita  (4) Satpratipakṣa

**5.** Assertion (A): “All things are non-eternal, because they are knowable” is an example of anupasaṃhārī savyabhicāra.
Reason (R): The pakṣa includes everything, so no sapakṣa or vipakṣa is left outside it against which the vyāpti could be checked.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true but (R) is false
(4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**6.** Statement I: Upamāna yields knowledge of the relation between a word and the object it denotes.
Statement II: The Vaiśeṣika school accepts upamāna as an independent pramāṇa.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true  (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**7.** “Wherever there is no fire, there is no smoke” expresses:
(1) Anvaya vyāpti  (2) Vyatireka vyāpti  (3) Sama vyāpti  (4) Upādhi

**8.** Which of the following are conditions of a valid hetu in Nyāya?
A. Pakṣadharmatā  B. Sapakṣasattva  C. Vipakṣāsattva  D. Savyabhicāra  E. Abādhitaviṣayatva
(1) A, B and C only  (2) A, B, C and E only  (3) A, C, D and E only  (4) A, B, C, D and E

**9.** Seeing a river in flood, one infers heavy rain in the hills. The kind of inference is:
(1) Pūrvavat  (2) Sāmānyatodṛṣṭa  (3) Śeṣavat  (4) Kevalānvayī

**10.** “Sound is eternal, because it is audible”, taken alone, is classed by Nyāya as asādhāraṇa savyabhicāra because:
