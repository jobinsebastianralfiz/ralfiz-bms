**4. (1)** R is Knowles’s experience assumption, and it is exactly why teachers invite adults to share experience (A).

**5. (1)** Subject mastery (A), communication (C) and the teacher’s expectations (D) belong to the teacher. Ventilation (B) is the physical learning environment; fee policy (E) is institutional.

**6. (2)** Tallies = 15 × 60 ÷ 3 = 300. Silence ratio = 30 ÷ 300 × 100 = 10%.

**7. (3)** Categories 1–4 are indirect, including asking questions (4). Giving directions is category 6, direct influence.

**8. (1)** From the base: direct purposeful, contrived (B), dramatised (D), demonstrations, study trips (A), exhibits (E), educational TV, motion pictures, recordings and pictures, visual symbols, verbal symbols (C).
