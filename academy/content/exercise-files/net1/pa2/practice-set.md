# Practice set 1.2: Learner characteristics and factors affecting teaching

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 8 minutes, problems 9–15 in 8 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

(1) Identity vs role confusion  (2) Intimacy vs isolation  (3) Generativity vs stagnation  (4) Industry vs inferiority

**2.** A student aged 9 years 6 months has a mental age of 11 years 5 months. What is the ratio IQ (to the nearest whole number)?
(1) 116  (2) 120  (3) 121  (4) 83

**3.** Match List I with List II.

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Personal fable | I. Marcia |
| B. Identity moratorium | II. Elkind |
| C. Storm and stress | III. Knowles |
| D. Andragogy | IV. G. Stanley Hall |

**4.** Assertion (A): Adult learners should be encouraged to share their work experiences in class.
Reason (R): In andragogy, the learner’s experience is treated as a rich resource for learning.

(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
