# Practice set 1.3: Teaching methods and support systems

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 8 minutes, problems 9–15 in 8 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

(1) Lecture method  (2) Heuristic method  (3) Recitation  (4) Narration
