# Solutions 1.3: Teaching methods and support systems

**1. Answer: (2)**
The heuristic method (H.E. Armstrong) places the learner in the position of a discoverer, so it builds the habit of finding out. Lecture, recitation and narration are teacher-centred and keep learners passive.

**2. Answer: (3)**
Symposium: several speakers, one topic, different aspects (A-III). Panel: experts talk informally before an audience with a moderator (B-IV). Workshop: hands-on and product-oriented (C-I). Seminar: one paper presented, then discussion (D-II). Trap: swapping symposium and panel, since both have several speakers. In a symposium each speaker gives a prepared talk; in a panel the experts converse.

**3. Answer: (2)**
Total telecast = 4 × 6 = 24 hours; first showings take 4 hours, so repeats take 24 − 4 = 20 hours. Trap: 24 is the whole day, and 5 is the number of repeats per programme, not hours.

**6. Answer: (2)**
AICTE (self-paced and international courses), IGNOU (out-of-school students), NITTTR (teacher training) and NIOS (school education) are national coordinators. NAAC is the accreditation body and has no SWAYAM coordination role.

**7. Answer: (4)**
Direct purposeful experiences (C) → contrived experiences (B) → exhibits (D) → visual symbols (A) → verbal symbols (E). Exhibits lie in the middle of the cone (after demonstrations and field trips); visual and verbal symbols are the two most abstract bands.

**8. Answer: (1)**

**9. Answer: (3)**
Networked, learner-generated knowledge with little central content is the connectivist model, the cMOOC. xMOOCs (Coursera, edX) are instructor-led; a SPOC is a Small Private Online Course with restricted enrolment.

**10. Answer: (4)**
Bloom–mastery learning (A-III); Aronson–jigsaw (B-IV); Keller–PSI (C-I); Osborn–brainstorming (D-II). Anchor the surest pair (Osborn with brainstorming) and only two options remain.

**11. Answer: (3)**

**15. Answer: (1)**
One skill, a few pupils, a few minutes and immediate feedback are the defining features of microteaching (Stanford, 1963). Team teaching involves several teachers and a full class; a symposium is a group presentation; the Dalton plan is about individual assignments.
