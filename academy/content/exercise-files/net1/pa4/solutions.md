# Solutions 1.4: Evaluation systems

**1. Answer: (1)**
Credit points: 4×8 + 3×10 + 3×7 + 2×5 + 2×9 = 32 + 30 + 21 + 10 + 18 = 111. Credits = 14. SGPA = 111 ÷ 14 = 7.928…, reported as 7.93. Trap: the unweighted mean (8 + 10 + 7 + 5 + 9) ÷ 5 = 7.80.

**2. Answer: (3)**
Rubric: criteria with described levels (A-IV). Portfolio: a collection showing growth (B-III). Sociometry: peer relationships, J.L. Moreno (C-II). Blueprint: the table of specifications for marks (D-I).

**3. Answer: (2)**
P = (30 + 14) ÷ 80 = 0.55. D = (30 − 14) ÷ 40 = 0.40, a very good discriminator. Trap: 0.44 divides 44 by 100 instead of by 2n = 80; 0.20 divides 16 by 80 (both groups) instead of by one group.

**4. Answer: (1)**
Relative grading places each learner according to the distribution of the group, which is exactly the norm-referenced idea. R states that mechanism, so it explains A.

**5. Answer: (4)**
Statement I is false: a checklist records only the presence or absence of a trait (yes/no); the degree is recorded by a rating scale. Statement II is the correct definition of an anecdotal record.

**6. Answer: (2)**
Test–retest, split-half, Kuder–Richardson and parallel forms all estimate consistency. Checking syllabus coverage (C) is content validity, not reliability.

**7. Answer: (3)**
Objectives in behavioural terms (B) → the test built from a blueprint (C) → administering (E) → scoring and interpreting (D) → feedback (A).

**8. Answer: (1)**
Σ(credits × SGPA) = 117.0 + 172.8 + 168.0 + 182.0 + 171.6 + 172.0 = 983.4; Σ credits = 130; CGPA = 983.4 ÷ 130 = 7.5646…, reported as 7.56. Trap: the unweighted mean of the SGPAs, 45.5 ÷ 6 = 7.58.

**9. Answer: (3)**
Percentile = 100 × 11,000 ÷ 12,500 = 88.0. Trap: 12.0 is the percentage of candidates above the candidate.

**10. Answer: (2)**
An on-demand examination (for example the NIOS On-Demand Examination System) lets learners appear when they are ready. CAT adapts the items, not the date; open-book and take-home exams change what may be consulted, not when the exam is taken.

**11. Answer: (4)**
A is false: an item that 95% answer correctly is too easy to separate strong from weak learners, so its D is close to 0. R is true, and it is the reason A fails.

**12. Answer: (3)**
r = 2 × 0.50 ÷ (1 + 0.50) = 1.00 ÷ 1.50 = 0.667 ≈ 0.67. Trap: 1.00 forgets the denominator.

**13. Answer: (1)**
A, B, D and E are correct. C is false: a DSE is an elective, chosen from a pool within the discipline; only Core courses are compulsory for all students.

**14. Answer: (2)**
Theory: 4 × 15 = 60 hours. Practicum: 2 × 30 = 60 hours. Total = 120 hours. Trap: 90 counts all 6 credits as theory (6 × 15).

**15. Answer: (4)**
Statement I is false: formative results are used for feedback and improvement during instruction; certification is summative. Statement II is true: summative evaluation is assessment of learning.
