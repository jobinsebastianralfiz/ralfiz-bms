# Practice set 1.4: Evaluation systems

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 8 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one. Use the UGC 10-point scale: O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, P 4, F 0.

(1) 7.93  (2) 7.80  (3) 8.00  (4) 7.40

| B. Portfolio | II. A mapping of peer relationships within a group |

**3.** With upper and lower groups of 40 students each, an item is answered correctly by 30 students of the upper group and 14 of the lower group. Find its difficulty index P and discrimination index D.
(1) P = 0.44, D = 0.40  (2) P = 0.55, D = 0.40  (3) P = 0.55, D = 0.20  (4) P = 0.40, D = 0.55
