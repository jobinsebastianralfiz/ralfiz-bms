# Practice set 6.2: Analogies, Venn diagrams and syllogisms

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 12 minutes, problems 9–15 in 12 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** Sculptor : Chisel :: Surgeon : ?
(1) Hospital  (2) Patient  (3) Scalpel  (4) Operation

**2.** 4 : 60 :: 6 : ?
(1) 180  (2) 210  (3) 216  (4) 222

**3.** BDF : YWU :: CEG : ?
(1) XVT  (2) XWT  (3) WVT  (4) XVS

**4.** Which diagram best represents Teachers, Graduates and Unemployed persons, given that every teacher is a graduate?
(1) Three nested circles  (2) Teachers inside Graduates, and Unemployed overlapping Graduates but not Teachers  (3) Three mutually overlapping circles  (4) Teachers inside Graduates, and Unemployed overlapping both

**5.** In a group of 80 scholars, 45 have published in journals, 38 have presented at conferences and 12 have done neither. How many have done both?
(1) 13  (2) 15  (3) 12  (4) 25

**6.** Of 150 students, 80 play cricket, 60 play football and 50 play hockey; 30 play cricket and football, 20 cricket and hockey, 15 football and hockey, and 5 play all three. How many play exactly one game?
(1) 70  (2) 75  (3) 90  (4) 130

**7.** Statements: Some pens are books. All books are papers.
Conclusions: I. Some pens are papers. II. All papers are books.
(1) Only conclusion I follows  (2) Only conclusion II follows  (3) Either conclusion I or II follows  (4) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

**8.** Statements: All roses are flowers. Some flowers are red.
Conclusions: I. Some roses are red. II. No rose is red.
(1) Only conclusion I follows  (2) Only conclusion II follows  (3) Either conclusion I or II follows  (4) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

**9.** Statements: No teacher is a lawyer. All lawyers are graduates.
Which of the following follow?
A. Some graduates are not teachers  B. No teacher is a graduate  C. Some graduates are lawyers  D. Some teachers are graduates  E. No lawyer is a teacher
(1) A, C and E only  (2) A and C only  (3) B, C and E only  (4) A, C, D and E only

**10.** Assertion (A): The syllogism “All M are P. All S are M. Therefore, some S are P.” is valid in the Boolean interpretation.
Reason (R): Its premises are both universal and its conclusion is particular.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true but (R) is false
(4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**11.** Statement I: In a Venn test, an X is placed on a line when the premise does not decide which of two unshaded regions is occupied.
Statement II: A syllogism is valid by the Venn test only if the conclusion has to be drawn separately after the premises.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true  (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**12.** Match List I (pair) with List II (relation).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Warm : Hot | I. Individual and group |
| B. Sheep : Flock | II. Study and subject |
| C. Seismology : Earthquakes | III. Milder and stronger degree |
| D. Wheat : Bread | IV. Raw material and product |

(1) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV  (2) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV  (3) A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**13.** Statements: All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are not employed.
Conclusions: I. Some engineers are not employed. II. All engineers being employed is a possibility.
(1) Only conclusion I follows  (2) Only conclusion II follows  (3) Both conclusions I and II follow  (4) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

**14.** In a class of 60, 42 like mathematics and 36 like physics. What is the maximum possible number who like neither?
(1) 0  (2) 6  (3) 18  (4) 24

**15.** Arrange the regions to be filled, in the correct order, when completing a three-set Venn diagram from given totals.
A. Region common to all three sets  B. Regions in exactly one set  C. Regions in exactly two sets  D. Region outside all sets
(1) A, C, B, D  (2) D, B, C, A  (3) A, B, C, D  (4) C, A, B, D
