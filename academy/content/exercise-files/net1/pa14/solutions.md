**7. Answer: (1)**

**8. Answer: (3)**
All + Some gives no definite link between roses and red things: the red flowers may or may not include roses. Neither conclusion follows definitely, but they have the same terms and form an I–E pair, so exactly one is true in every case: either I or II.
