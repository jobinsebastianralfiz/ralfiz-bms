# Practice set 5.1: Reasoning: series, codes and relationships

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** What is the next term of the series 6, 11, 21, 36, 56, ?
(1) 76  (2) 81  (3) 86  (4) 71

**2.** Find the wrong term in the series 1, 4, 9, 16, 24, 36, 49.
(1) 16  (2) 24  (3) 36  (4) 9

**3.** What is the next term of the series 2, 10, 4, 20, 8, 40, ?
(1) 16  (2) 80  (3) 12  (4) 32

**4.** What is the next term of the series 0, 7, 26, 63, 124, ?
(1) 215  (2) 216  (3) 217  (4) 225

**5.** What comes next in the letter series C, H, M, R, ?
(1) V  (2) W  (3) X  (4) U

**6.** Which letter is the 8th to the left of the 20th letter from the left in the English alphabet?
(1) K  (2) M  (3) L  (4) B

**7.** In a certain code, WATER is written as YCVGT. How is PLANT written in that code?
(1) RNCPV  (2) RNCOV  (3) QMBOU  (4) RMCPV

**8.** If each letter is replaced by its opposite letter (A ↔ Z, B ↔ Y, and so on), which word is coded as HLFGS?
(1) SOUTH  (2) SOUTE  (3) MOUTH  (4) SOFTH

**9.** In a certain code, BEAD = 12. Using the same rule, what is the code for FACE?
(1) 14  (2) 15  (3) 16  (4) 17

**10.** Pointing to a woman, Rahul says, “She is the daughter of the only child of my grandmother.” How is the woman related to Rahul?
(1) Cousin  (2) Aunt  (3) Sister  (4) Niece

**11.** A × B means A is the mother of B; A + B means A is the brother of B; A − B means A is the husband of B. If M + N × P − Q, how is M related to P?
(1) Father  (2) Uncle  (3) Brother  (4) Cousin

**12.** A man walks 20 m west, turns right and walks 10 m, turns right and walks 35 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. How far is he from the start?
(1) 15 m  (2) 25 m  (3) 20 m  (4) 35 m

**13.** In the evening, Leela is walking and her shadow falls exactly in front of her. In which direction is she walking?
(1) North  (2) South  (3) West  (4) East

**14.** Five people sit in a row facing north. B is to the immediate right of D. E is at the left end. A is between E and D. C is at the right end. Arrange them from left to right.
A. A  B. B  C. C  D. D  E. E
(1) E, A, D, B, C  (2) E, D, A, B, C  (3) A, E, D, B, C  (4) E, A, B, D, C

**15.** Which of the following statements are correct?
A. The next term of 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 is 13.
B. The 10th letter from the right end of the alphabet is Q.
C. If a person is 7th from the left and 9th from the right, the row has 16 people.
D. A sound argument is valid and has true premises.
E. Every inductive conclusion is certain.
(1) A, B and D only  (2) A, B, C and D only  (3) A and D only  (4) B, C and D only
