# Solutions: practice set 5.1

**1. Answer: (2)**
Differences: 5, 10, 15, 20 (multiples of 5). The next difference is 25, so 56 + 25 = 81.

**2. Answer: (2)**
The series is the squares 1², 2², …, 7². The fifth term should be 5² = 25, not 24.

**3. Answer: (1)**
Two interleaved series. Odd positions: 2, 4, 8, … (× 2), so the seventh term is 16. Even positions: 10, 20, 40 (× 2). The question asks for the seventh term, which is in the odd series.

**4. Answer: (1)**
The terms are n³ − 1: 1 − 1 = 0, 8 − 1 = 7, 27 − 1 = 26, 64 − 1 = 63, 125 − 1 = 124, so the next is 216 − 1 = 215. The option 216 forgets the − 1.

**5. Answer: (2)**
Values 3, 8, 13, 18: add 5 each time, so the next is 23 = W.

**6. Answer: (3)**
The 20th letter is T (EJOTY anchor). Eight to the left: 20 − 8 = 12 = L.

**7. Answer: (1)**
Each letter moves two places forward: W → Y, A → C, T → V, E → G, R → T. So P → R, L → N, A → C, N → P, T → V: RNCPV.

**8. Answer: (1)**
Decode by taking opposite letters again (the rule is its own inverse): H (8) → 19 = S, L → O, F (6) → 21 = U, G (7) → 20 = T, S (19) → 8 = H. The word is SOUTH.

**9. Answer: (2)**
BEAD = 2 + 5 + 1 + 4 = 12, the sum of position values. FACE = 6 + 1 + 3 + 5 = 15.

**10. Answer: (3)**
The only child of Rahul’s grandmother must be Rahul’s own parent. The daughter of Rahul’s parent is Rahul’s sister.

**11. Answer: (2)**
M is the brother of N; N is the mother of P; P is the husband of Q. So M is the brother of P’s mother, that is P’s (maternal) uncle.

**12. Answer: (1)**
West 20 → (−20, 0). A right turn from west faces north: 10 → (−20, 10). A right turn from north faces east: 35 → (15, 10). A right turn from east faces south: 10 → (15, 0). He is 15 m east of the start. The path length, 75 m, is not asked.

**13. Answer: (4)**
In the evening the sun is in the west, so shadows fall to the east. Her shadow is in front of her, so she is walking east.

**14. Answer: (1)**
E is at position 1 and C at position 5. A lies between E and D, and B is immediately to the right of D, so the middle three places are A, D, B. Order from left to right: E, A, D, B, C.

**15. Answer: (1)**
A is true (Fibonacci: 5 + 8 = 13). B is true (27 − 10 = 17 = Q). C is false: 7 + 9 − 1 = 15, not 16. D is true (the definition of soundness). E is false: inductive conclusions are only probable.
