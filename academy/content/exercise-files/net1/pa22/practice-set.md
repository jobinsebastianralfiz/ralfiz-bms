# Practice set 9.3: Environmental protection acts and international agreements

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** Arrange in chronological order (earliest first).
A. Nagoya Protocol adopted  B. Cartagena Protocol adopted  C. CBD opened for signature  D. Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework adopted  E. CBD enters into force
(1) C, E, B, A, D  (2) C, B, E, A, D  (3) E, C, B, A, D  (4) C, E, A, B, D

**2.** Match List I (agreement) with List II (date of entry into force).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. UNFCCC | I. 1 January 2019 |
| B. Kyoto Protocol | II. 16 February 2005 |
| C. Paris Agreement | III. 21 March 1994 |
| D. Kigali Amendment | IV. 4 November 2016 |

(1) A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I  (2) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I  (3) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I

**3.** Statement I: The NAPCC was released on 30 June 2008. Statement II: The NAPCC has ten national missions.
(1) Both statements are true  (2) Both statements are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**4.** A country’s HFC baseline is 150 Mt CO₂e. Under India’s Kigali schedule (cuts of 10%, 20%, 30% and 85% from the baseline in 2032, 2037, 2042 and 2047), its maximum consumption in 2037 is:
(1) 30 Mt  (2) 108 Mt  (3) 120 Mt  (4) 135 Mt

**5.** Which gases were in the basket of the Kyoto Protocol’s first commitment period?
A. Carbon dioxide  B. Methane  C. CFC-11  D. Sulphur hexafluoride  E. Nitrous oxide
(1) A, B and E only  (2) A, B, D and E only  (3) A, B, C, D and E  (4) A, C, D and E only

**6.** Assertion (A): The ISA became a treaty-based international organisation in December 2017. Reason (R): Its Framework Agreement entered into force on the thirtieth day after the fifteenth ratification, which came in November 2017.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is true but (R) is false  (4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**7.** The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 was enacted under which Article of the Constitution?
(1) Article 48A  (2) Article 51A(g)  (3) Article 253  (4) Article 21

**8.** Match List I (section of the EPA, 1986) with List II (content).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
