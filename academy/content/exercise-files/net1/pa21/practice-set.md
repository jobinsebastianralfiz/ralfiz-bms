# Practice set 9.2: Pollution, resources and disasters

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 12 minutes, problems 9–15 in 12 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** Three identical fans each produce 70 dB at a point. Running together, the level is closest to:
(1) 73.0 dB  (2) 74.8 dB  (3) 76.0 dB  (4) 210 dB

**2.** PM10 at a station is 300 µg/m³ (24-hour). In India’s AQI, PM10 of 251–350 corresponds to sub-index 201–300. The PM10 sub-index (rounded) is:
(1) 249  (2) 250  (3) 251  (4) 300

**3.** Match List I (waste) with List II (bag or container colour under the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Human anatomical waste | I. White (translucent), puncture-proof |
| B. Contaminated recyclable plastics (tubing, bottles) | II. Blue |
| C. Needles and other sharps | III. Yellow |
| D. Glassware | IV. Red |

(1) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II  (2) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II  (3) A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**4.** Statement I: CO₂ is one of the eight pollutants of India’s National AQI. Statement II: The overall AQI is the highest of the pollutant sub-indices.
(1) Both statements are true  (2) Both statements are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**5.** A wind turbine produces 200 kW at 6 m/s. Other things equal, at 9 m/s it produces about:
(1) 300 kW  (2) 450 kW  (3) 675 kW  (4) 900 kW

**6.** Which of the following are non-structural measures of disaster mitigation?
A. Seismic building codes enforced through approvals  B. Early warning systems and evacuation drills  C. Retrofitting old bridges  D. Crop and property insurance  E. Check dams
(1) A, B and D only  (2) B, C and D only  (3) A, B, D and E only  (4) B and D only

**7.** An industry emits 50 t CH₄ in a year. With a GWP of 28, this equals how many tonnes of CO₂-equivalent?
(1) 78  (2) 560  (3) 1,400  (4) 14,000

**8.** Assertion (A): Arsenic contamination of groundwater is a serious problem in parts of the Ganga–Brahmaputra delta. Reason (R): Arsenic occurs naturally in the sediments of the delta and dissolves into groundwater drawn by tube wells.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is true but (R) is false  (4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**9.** Arrange the AQI categories in order of increasing pollution.
A. Poor  B. Satisfactory  C. Severe  D. Moderately polluted  E. Very poor
(1) B, D, A, E, C  (2) B, A, D, E, C  (3) D, B, A, E, C  (4) B, D, E, A, C

**10.** A sample diluted to 5% (P = 0.05) shows DO falling from 8.0 to 5.0 mg/L in 5 days. BOD₅ is:
(1) 3 mg/L  (2) 15 mg/L  (3) 60 mg/L  (4) 150 mg/L

**11.** Match List I (event) with List II (pollutant).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Bhopal gas tragedy | I. Cadmium |
| B. Minamata disease | II. Methyl isocyanate |
| C. Itai-itai disease | III. Sulphur dioxide and coal smoke |
| D. London smog of 1952 | IV. Methyl mercury |

(1) A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III  (2) A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III  (3) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III  (4) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

**12.** Which Framework for disaster risk reduction preceded the Sendai Framework?
(1) Yokohama Strategy  (2) Hyogo Framework for Action  (3) Kyoto Framework  (4) Agenda 21

**13.** Statement I: Biomass is a renewable energy source if the plants used are regrown. Statement II: Geothermal energy is derived from the heat of the Earth’s interior.
(1) Both statements are true  (2) Both statements are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**14.** A hydro scheme has Q = 10 m³/s, H = 100 m and η = 0.8 (ρ = 1,000 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s²). Its output is about:
(1) 7.85 MW  (2) 9.81 MW  (3) 0.785 MW  (4) 78.5 MW

**15.** Arrange in increasing order of permissible night-time noise limit (Noise Rules, 2000).
A. Commercial area  B. Silence zone  C. Industrial area  D. Residential area
(1) B, D, A, C  (2) D, B, A, C  (3) B, A, D, C  (4) B, D, C, A
