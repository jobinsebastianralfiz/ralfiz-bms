# Practice set 7.1: Data: sources, classification and representation

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 12 minutes, problems 9–15 in 12 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** A researcher uses the unit-level data of a national household survey, downloaded from the government’s statistics portal, for her thesis. For her, these data are:
(1) Primary data  (2) Secondary data  (3) Experimental data  (4) Qualitative data

**2.** Which average is meaningful for the variable “colour of a car”?
(1) Mean  (2) Median  (3) Mode  (4) Geometric mean

**3.** In a pie chart showing a household budget of ₹48,000, the sector for education is 60°. The amount spent on education is:
(1) ₹6,000  (2) ₹8,000  (3) ₹12,000  (4) ₹9,600

**4.** In an inclusive distribution with classes 5–9, 10–14, 15–19, …, the class mark and the upper boundary of the class 10–14 are:
(1) 12 and 14  (2) 12.5 and 14.5  (3) 12 and 14.5  (4) 12 and 15

**5.** The frequencies of the classes 0–10, 10–20 and 20–50 are 8, 14 and 18. If the histogram uses frequency density (per mark), the heights of the three bars are:
(1) 0.8, 1.4 and 0.6  (2) 8, 14 and 18  (3) 0.8, 1.4 and 1.8  (4) 0.8, 1.4 and 0.36

**6.** Daily wages (₹) of 60 workers: 100–200: 8, 200–300: 14, 300–400: 20, 400–500: 10, 500–600: 8. The median wage is:
(1) ₹343.33  (2) ₹350  (3) ₹340  (4) ₹330

**7.** For the same data as problem 6, the mean wage (to two decimals) is:
(1) ₹340.00  (2) ₹343.33  (3) ₹350.00  (4) ₹336.67

**9.** Match List I with List II.

| List I (Situation) | List II (Diagram) |

**10.** Statement I: A dot map is best for showing the distribution of population, with each dot standing for a fixed number of people.
Statement II: An isopleth map shades each district according to its literacy rate.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
(2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
(3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
(4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**11.** Assertion (A): The less-than and more-than ogives of a distribution intersect at the median.
Reason (R): At the median, the number of observations below it equals the number above it.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true but (R) is false
(4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**12.** Which of the following are parts of a statistical table? A. Stub  B. Caption  C. Legend of a map  D. Source note  E. Body
(1) A, B and E only  (2) A, B, D and E only  (3) B, C and D only  (4) A, B, C, D and E

**13.** Arrange in the correct order the steps of drawing a pie chart from raw values: A. Draw the sectors with a protractor  B. Find the total  C. Convert each value to degrees  D. Label each sector with its percentage
(1) B, C, A, D  (2) C, B, A, D  (3) B, A, C, D  (4) B, C, D, A

**14.** A frequency distribution has 7 classes of equal width. The smallest value is 12 and the largest is 82, and the classes start at 12 and together cover exactly the range. The class width is:
(1) 7  (2) 12  (3) 10  (4) 11.7
