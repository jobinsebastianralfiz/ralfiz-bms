# Solutions: Practice set 2.2

**1. Answer: (1)**
Literature survey (C), working hypotheses (B), research design (D), sample design (A), data collection (E). In Kothari’s order the research design comes before the sample design.

**2. Answer: (2)**
k = 2,000 ÷ 50 = 40. Selected: 13, 53, 93, … The third is 13 + 2 × 40 = 93. 53 is the second and 133 the fourth.

**3. Answer: (3)**
Each respondent introduces further respondents: snowball (chain-referral) sampling, the usual choice for hidden populations.

**4. Answer: (3)**
α is the probability of a Type I error, so lowering it reduces Type I errors (true). With the sample size fixed, a stricter α makes it harder to reject a false H₀, so β rises (Statement II false). Only a larger sample reduces both.

**5. Answer: (1)**
Questionnaire: respondent fills it (II). Schedule: enumerator fills it (IV). Sociometry: choices within a group (I). Delphi: anonymous expert rounds (III).

**6. Answer: (3)**
Full test: r = 2 × 0.50 ÷ 1.50 = 0.667. Three times as long: 3 × 0.667 ÷ (1 + 2 × 0.667) = 2.0 ÷ 2.333 = 0.857 ≈ 0.86. Option (1), 0.75, lengthens the half-test by 3 instead of the full test.

**7. Answer: (2)**
The year of study changes the strength of the yoga–anxiety relationship: a moderator. It is not the outcome (dependent), not an unobserved process linking cause and effect (intervening), and the researcher wants to study it rather than remove it.

**8. Answer: (1)**
Both are true, and R is the reason: since the enumerator reads the questions aloud and records the answers, the respondent does not need to read.

**9. Answer: (2)**
Non-response (A), a faulty frame (B), leading questions (D) and data-entry mistakes (E) are non-sampling errors. The chance difference between statistic and parameter (C) is sampling error.

**10. Answer: (4)**
“Twice as many” needs a true zero: ratio scale. Marks out of a total have a true zero (zero marks means none scored).

**11. Answer: (3)**
The criterion (grades) is obtained later, so this is predictive validity. Concurrent validity would use a criterion measured at the same time.

**12. Answer: (2)**
Fraction = 150 ÷ 3,000 = 0.05. Private: 600 × 0.05 = 30 (government 120).

**13. Answer: (4)**
Strata should be homogeneous within (Statement I false); clusters should ideally be heterogeneous within, each a miniature of the population (Statement II true).

**14. Answer: (4)**
κ = (0.80 − 0.60) ÷ (1 − 0.60) = 0.20 ÷ 0.40 = 0.50. 0.20 forgets the denominator.

**15. Answer: (4)**
Type I: rejecting a true H₀ (II). Type II: failing to reject a false H₀ (IV). Power: probability of rejecting a false H₀ (I). Level of significance: the accepted probability of a Type I error (III).
