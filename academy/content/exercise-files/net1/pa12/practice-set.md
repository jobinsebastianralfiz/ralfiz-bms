# Practice set 5.2: Mathematical aptitude

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. No calculator. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** What is 15% of 40% of 2,500?
(1) 100  (2) 150  (3) 175  (4) 1,375

**2.** A number is increased by 20% and the result is decreased by 25%. What is the net change?
(1) 5% decrease  (2) 10% decrease  (3) 5% increase  (4) No change

**3.** An article bought for ₹ 480 is sold for ₹ 552. What is the profit percentage?
(1) 13.04%  (2) 12%  (3) 15%  (4) 72%

**4.** The marked price of a watch is ₹ 1,200. It is sold after successive discounts of 10% and 5%. What is the selling price?
(1) ₹ 1,020  (2) ₹ 1,026  (3) ₹ 1,030  (4) ₹ 1,080

**5.** What is the simple interest on ₹ 7,500 at 6% per annum for 2 1/2 years?
(1) ₹ 900  (2) ₹ 1,050  (3) ₹ 1,125  (4) ₹ 1,200

**6.** What is the compound interest on ₹ 20,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually?
(1) ₹ 2,000  (2) ₹ 2,050  (3) ₹ 2,100  (4) ₹ 2,025

**7.** What is the difference between the compound interest (compounded annually) and the simple interest on ₹ 1,000 at 10% per annum for 3 years?
(1) ₹ 10  (2) ₹ 21  (3) ₹ 31  (4) ₹ 33.10

**8.** A train 180 m long passes a man walking at 6 km/h in the same direction as the train in 12 seconds. What is the speed of the train?
(1) 54 km/h  (2) 48 km/h  (3) 66 km/h  (4) 60 km/h

**9.** A boat covers 30 km downstream in 2 hours and 18 km upstream in 2 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(1) 12 km/h  (2) 15 km/h  (3) 9 km/h  (4) 3 km/h

**10.** The average weight of 8 persons rises by 2.5 kg when a person weighing 65 kg is replaced by a new person. What is the weight of the new person?
(1) 67.5 kg  (2) 75 kg  (3) 85 kg  (4) 80 kg

**11.** ₹ 4,800 is divided between A and B in the ratio 5 : 7. What is B’s share?
(1) ₹ 2,000  (2) ₹ 2,800  (3) ₹ 3,360  (4) ₹ 2,400

**12.** Tea costing ₹ 120 per kg is mixed with tea costing ₹ 150 per kg in the ratio 2 : 3. What is the cost price of the mixture per kg?
(1) ₹ 135  (2) ₹ 138  (3) ₹ 140  (4) ₹ 132

**13.** On a certain sum due at a future date, the banker’s discount is ₹ 120 and the true discount is ₹ 100 (same rate and time). What is the sum?
(1) ₹ 500  (2) ₹ 720  (3) ₹ 600  (4) ₹ 1,200

**14.** Arrange the fractions in increasing order.
A. 2/3  B. 3/5  C. 5/8  D. 4/7  E. 7/12
(1) D, B, E, C, A  (2) D, E, B, C, A  (3) E, D, B, C, A  (4) D, E, C, B, A

**15.** Which of the following statements are correct?
A. 1/6 is equal to 16.67% (to two decimal places).
B. 12.5% is equal to 1/8.
C. If A is 20% more than B, then B is 20% less than A.
D. The compound interest for 2 years at 10% per annum (compounded annually) is 21% of the principal.
E. The average speed for equal distances at 30 km/h and 20 km/h is 25 km/h.
(1) A, B and D only  (2) A, B, C and D only  (3) A and B only  (4) B, D and E only
