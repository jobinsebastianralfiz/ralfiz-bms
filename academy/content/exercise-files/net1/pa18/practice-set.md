# Practice set 8.1: ICT basics and terminology

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 10 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** In the binary convention, 4 GB is equal to:
(1) 4000 MB  (2) 4096 MB  (3) 4,194,304 MB  (4) 1024 MB

**2.** The decimal number 45 in hexadecimal is:
(1) 2D  (2) 2B  (3) 55  (4) D2

**3.** (110110)₂ in octal is:
(1) 54  (2) 66  (3) 36  (4) 63

**4.** A 1.5 GB video (take 1 GB = 1000 MB) is downloaded over a 100 Mbps connection. Ignoring overheads, the time taken is:
(1) 15 seconds  (2) 120 seconds  (3) 12 seconds  (4) 150 seconds

**5.** The decimal fraction 0.625 in binary is:
(1) 0.110  (2) 0.011  (3) 0.101  (4) 0.1001

**6.** (ABC)₁₆ in decimal is:
(1) 2748  (2) 2478  (3) 2847  (4) 3276

**7.** Match List I with List II.

| List I (Abbreviation) | List II (Full form) |
|---|---|
| A. MICR | I. Hypertext Markup Language |
| B. HTML | II. Magnetic Ink Character Recognition |
| C. VoIP | III. Universal Serial Bus |
| D. USB | IV. Voice over Internet Protocol |

(1) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III  (2) A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III  (3) A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III  (4) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

**8.** Which of the following are output devices?
A. Plotter  B. Light pen  C. Speaker  D. Projector  E. Joystick
(1) A, C and D only  (2) A, B and C only  (3) B and E only  (4) A, C, D and E only

**9.** Assertion (A): A company lets its dealers log in to check stock levels in its private network; this is an extranet.
Reason (R): An extranet is an intranet that is extended, with controlled access, to selected outside users.
(1) Both true, R explains A  (2) Both true, R does not explain A  (3) A true, R false  (4) A false, R true

**10.** Statement I: SMTP is used to retrieve mail from a server to a client.
Statement II: IMAP keeps messages on the server, so they stay synchronised across devices.
(1) Both true  (2) Both false  (3) I true, II false  (4) I false, II true

**11.** Arrange in decreasing order of size: A. 1 TB  B. 1 PB  C. 1,000,000 KB (decimal)  D. 1 GB (binary)  E. 1 EB
(1) E, B, A, D, C  (2) E, B, A, C, D  (3) B, E, A, D, C  (4) E, A, B, D, C

**12.** How many different values can be represented with 12 bits?
(1) 4095  (2) 1024  (3) 2048  (4) 4096

**13.** A lecturer puts her head of department in CC and 60 students in BCC of an e-mail. A student clicks “Reply All”. Who receives the reply?
(1) All 60 students and the lecturer  (2) The lecturer and the head of department  (3) Only the lecturer  (4) Everyone, including all BCC recipients

**14.** Which of the following is a language translator that works on one statement at a time?
(1) Compiler  (2) Assembler  (3) Linker  (4) Interpreter

**15.** Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(1) Router: joins devices of one LAN only  (2) DNS: converts domain names to IP addresses  (3) HTTP: sends e-mail  (4) ROM: volatile memory
