# Practice set 2.3: Thesis writing, ICT in research and ethics

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 11 minutes, problems 9–15 in 10 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** Which of the following is an APA 7 in-text citation of page 12 of a work by two authors, Sharma and Iyer (2021)?
(1) (Sharma and Iyer 12)  (2) (Sharma & Iyer, 2021, p. 12)  (3) (Sharma and Iyer 2021, 12)  (4) (Sharma, Iyer, 2021: 12)

**2.** Footnote 7 cites Kumar, page 30. Footnote 8 cites the same book, page 34. According to the traditional system, footnote 8 is written:
(1) Op. cit., 34  (2) Loc. cit.  (3) Ibid., 34  (4) Et al., 34

**3.** Match List I (service) with List II (function).

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. Shodhganga | I. Synopses of research proposals |
| B. Shodhgangotri | II. Plagiarism detection software for institutions |
| C. ShodhShuddhi | III. Electronic theses and dissertations |
| D. VIDWAN | IV. Database of experts |

(1) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II  (2) A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV  (3) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV  (4) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV

**4.** A PhD thesis shows 55% similarity after exclusions. Under the UGC 2018 regulations, the scholar:
(1) Must revise the thesis within six months  (2) Is debarred from submitting a revised script for one year  (3) Has the registration cancelled  (4) Faces no penalty

**5.** Citations of eight papers: 20, 14, 9, 8, 5, 5, 2, 0. The h-index is:
(1) 4  (2) 5  (3) 6  (4) 8

**6.** Statement I: MLA 9 places the year of publication in parentheses immediately after the author’s name in the Works Cited list.
Statement II: MLA 9 places the title of an article in quotation marks.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true  (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**7.** Which of the following are forms of plagiarism? A. Copying text word for word without quotation marks or citation B. Paraphrasing another author’s argument without citation C. Reusing one’s own published text without disclosure D. Quoting a source with quotation marks and a full citation E. Translating another author’s text and presenting it as one’s own
(1) A, B, C and E only  (2) A, B and E only  (3) A, C, D and E only  (4) B, C and D only

**8.** Assertion (A): An author’s g-index is always at least as large as the h-index.
Reason (R): If h papers each have at least h citations, then the top h papers together have at least h² citations.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is true but (R) is false  (4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**9.** A researcher changes the date on some survey forms so that they appear to have been collected within the approved period, and drops three villages whose data contradict her hypothesis. This is:
(1) Fabrication  (2) Falsification  (3) Plagiarism  (4) Gift authorship

**10.** Arrange the chapters of the main body of a thesis in the usual order: A. Discussion B. Review of literature C. Introduction D. Results and analysis E. Research methodology
(1) C, B, E, D, A  (2) C, E, B, D, A  (3) B, C, E, D, A  (4) C, B, D, E, A

**11.** A journal’s 2025 Impact Factor is based on 200 citable items published in 2023 and 2024, which were cited 540 times in 2025. The Impact Factor is:
(1) 2.0  (2) 5.4  (3) 2.7  (4) 0.37

**12.** Statement I: In double-blind peer review, the reviewers do not know who the authors are, and the authors do not know who the reviewers are.
Statement II: In single-blind peer review, the authors know the identity of the reviewers but the reviewers do not know the authors.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true  (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false  (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true
