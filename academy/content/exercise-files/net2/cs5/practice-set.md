# Practice set 1.5: Group theory

Time yourself: problems 1–8 in 12 minutes, problems 9–15 in 12 minutes. Every question has four options; choose one.

**1.** The number of generators of the cyclic group (ℤ₁₈, +₁₈) is:
(1) 17  (2) 6  (3) 8  (4) 9

**2.** The order of the element 4 in (ℤ₁₈, +₁₈) is:
(1) 4  (2) 18  (3) 9  (4) 3

**3.** In U(15) = {1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14} under multiplication modulo 15, the order of 2 is:
(1) 4  (2) 8  (3) 2  (4) 15

**4.** The number of subgroups of (ℤ₂₀, +₂₀) is:
(1) 8  (2) 4  (3) 5  (4) 6

**5.** On ℤ, define a * b = a + b + 3. (ℤ, *) is a group. The inverse of 4 is:
(1) −4  (2) −7  (3) −10  (4) −1

**6.** A group of order 42 has a subgroup H of order 6. The number of left cosets of H is:
(1) 6  (2) 7  (3) 36  (4) 42

**7.** The order of the permutation (1 3 5)(2 4 6 7 8) in S₈ is:
(1) 8  (2) 5  (3) 15  (4) 3

**8.** The number of elements of order 5 in (ℤ₂₅, +₂₅) is:
(1) 4  (2) 5  (3) 20  (4) 1

**9.** The number of non-zero zero divisors in the ring ℤ₂₀ is:
(1) 8  (2) 12  (3) 10  (4) 11

**10.** Match List I (set and operation) with List II (strongest structure it forms). Here ℕ = {1, 2, 3, …}.

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| A. (ℕ, +) | I. Abelian group |
| B. (ℕ ∪ {0}, +) | II. Only a groupoid (closed but not associative) |
| C. (ℤ, +) | III. Semigroup, not a monoid |
| D. (ℤ, −) | IV. Monoid, not a group |

(1) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II  (2) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II  (3) A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I  (4) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

**11.** Assertion (A): U(8) = {1, 3, 5, 7} under multiplication modulo 8 is not cyclic.
Reason (R): Every non-identity element of U(8) has order 2.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true but (R) is false
(4) (A) is false but (R) is true

**12.** Statement I: Every group of order 6 is abelian.
Statement II: Every group of order 5 is cyclic.
(1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
(2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
(3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
(4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**13.** Which of the following are subgroups of (ℤ₁₂, +₁₂)?

(1) A, B and C only  (2) A, B, C and E only  (3) A, B, C, D and E  (4) B, C and E only

(1) 1  (2) 4  (3) 3  (4) 12
