# Solutions: Practice set 1.6

**1.** Σ deg = 2 × 35 = 70 = 4 × 5 + 5 × 4 + 3r = 40 + 3r, so r = 10 vertices of degree 3. Total n = 4 + 5 + 10 = 19. Trap: 10 is only the degree-3 vertices. **Answer: (2) 19**

**2.** e = 20 × 3 / 2 = 30. f = 2 − v + e = 2 − 20 + 30 = 12 (the dodecahedron). Trap: forgetting the outer face gives 11. **Answer: (3) 12**

**3.** Cayley: nⁿ⁻² = 6⁴ = 1296. Trap: 7776 = 6⁵ and 216 = 6³ use the wrong exponent; 720 = 6!. **Answer: (2) 1296**

**4.** The rim is an odd cycle, which needs 3 colours; the hub is adjacent to every rim vertex, so it needs a fourth. χ = 4. With an even rim the answer would be 3. **Answer: (3) 4**

**5.** (n − k)(n − k + 1)/2 = 8 × 9 / 2 = 36: K₉ plus three isolated vertices. Trap: 45 = C(10, 2) is for 3 components; 66 = C(12, 2) is K₁₂. **Answer: (1) 36**

**6.** Exactly two odd-degree vertices (the two of degree 3), so there is an Euler path between them but no Euler circuit. Degrees say nothing definite about Hamiltonicity. **Answer: (2) It has an Euler path but no Euler circuit**

**7.** Qₙ has n·2ⁿ⁻¹ edges: 5 × 16 = 80. Trap: 32 = 2⁵ is the vertex count; 160 forgets to halve the degree sum. **Answer: (3) 80**

**8.** (n − 1)!/2 = 6!/2 = 360. Trap: 720 counts each cycle in both directions. **Answer: (4) 360**

**9.** P₄ is a tree on 4 vertices: P(k) = k(k − 1)³, so P(3) = 3 × 8 = 24. Trap: 18 is the value for the cycle C₄. **Answer: (1) 24**

**10.** τ(K₄) = 4² = 16 (III); τ(C₇) = 7 (I); τ(K₂,₄) = 2³ × 4¹ = 32 (IV); τ(K₅) = 5³ = 125 (II). **Answer: (1) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II**

**11.** Q₃ is 3-regular, so every vertex has odd degree, and Euler’s theorem rules out an Euler circuit (and, with 8 odd vertices, even an Euler path). R explains A. **Answer: (1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)**

**12.** Statement I follows from Euler’s formula with 3f ≤ 2e. Statement II is the false converse: K₃,₃ has v = 6 and e = 9 ≤ 12 but is non-planar. **Answer: (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false**

**13.** A–D are equivalent characterisations of trees. E is false: a tree with at least one edge is bipartite, so χ = 2. **Answer: (2) A, B, C and D only**

**14.** C₉: 9; K₃,₄: 12; K₆: 15; wheel with 8 rim vertices: 16; Q₄: 32. Increasing: C, B, A, E, D. **Answer: (1) C, B, A, E, D**

**15.** (2n − 1)!! for K₂ₙ: 7 × 5 × 3 × 1 = 105. Trap: 28 = C(8, 2) is the edge count; 40320 = 8! counts orderings. **Answer: (2) 105**
