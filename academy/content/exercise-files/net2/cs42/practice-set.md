# Practice set: Software quality (cs42)

Time: 20 minutes. Give numerical answers to the precision asked. A year has 8760 hours. Components fail independently.

1. A system has MTTF = 1200 hours and MTTR = 30 hours. Find its availability as a percentage, to two decimal places.

2. A router has MTBF = 800 hours and MTTR = 8 hours. Find its availability.

3. A service has MTTF = 995 hours. What is the largest MTTR (in hours) that still gives at least 99.5% availability?

4. Three components are in series with reliabilities 0.98, 0.97 and 0.99. Find the system reliability to three decimal places.

5. Two identical servers (each with reliability 0.8) work in parallel, and the pair is in series with a storage unit of reliability 0.95. Find the system reliability.

6. A module has a constant failure rate of 0.001 failures per hour. Find (a) its MTTF and (b) the probability that it runs 500 hours without failure, to four decimal places.

7. Under Musa’s basic execution-time model, λ₀ = 15 failures per CPU hour and ν₀ = 150 failures. So far 60 failures have been experienced.
   (a) What is the present failure intensity?
   (b) How many more failures must be experienced to reach 3 failures per CPU hour?
   (c) How much more execution time is needed for that, to two decimal places?

8. What is the maximum yearly downtime, in minutes, for a system with 99.99% availability?

9. A team found 120 defects before release, and customers found 30 after release. Find the defect removal efficiency.

10. 24 defects are found in 800 printed invoices. Each invoice has 5 opportunities for a defect. Find the DPMO.

11. An 80 KLOC system took 400 person-months to build. Each year 6 KLOC is added and 10 KLOC is modified. Using annual change traffic, estimate the yearly maintenance effort.

12. Match List I with List II.

| List I (Maintenance) | List II (Example) |
|---|---|
| A. Corrective | I. Migrating the app to a new cloud database service |
| B. Adaptive | II. Rewriting a module with clearer structure before it causes problems |
| C. Perfective | III. Fixing wrong totals reported by a user |
| D. Preventive | IV. Adding export to PDF on user request |

Options: (1) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II (2) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II (3) A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II (4) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV

13. Assertion (A): MTBF is always larger than MTTF for a repairable system.
Reason (R): MTBF = MTTF + MTTR, and MTTR is positive.
Options: (1) Both true, R explains A (2) Both true, R does not explain A (3) A true, R false (4) A false, R true

14. Which of the following are key process areas of CMM level 3 (Defined)?
A. Organisation process definition  B. Training program  C. Peer reviews  D. Quantitative process management  E. Intergroup coordination
Options: (1) A, B and C only (2) A, B, C and E only (3) B, C, D and E only (4) A, C and E only

15. Arrange the steps of a Fagan inspection in order: A. Inspection meeting, B. Follow-up, C. Planning, D. Preparation, E. Rework.
Options: (1) C, D, A, E, B (2) C, A, D, E, B (3) D, C, A, E, B (4) C, D, A, B, E
