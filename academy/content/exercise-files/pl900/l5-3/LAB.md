## Steps

2. In your agent, open Knowledge > Add knowledge and upload it-faq.md. If the file type is refused, save it as .txt or .docx first. Wait until it shows as ready.
3. Add your college’s public website URL as a second knowledge source.
4. In the agent’s settings, turn off the option to use general knowledge, and save.
5. In the test pane, ask questions 1 to 6 from knowledge-test-questions.md and click each citation.
6. Ask question 7 to test the website source.
7. Ask questions 8 and 9 and note what the agent does when no source covers a question.
