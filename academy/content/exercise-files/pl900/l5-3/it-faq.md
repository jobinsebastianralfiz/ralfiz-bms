# Campus Help Desk: IT FAQ

Last updated: 1 September 2026. Owner: Campus Help Desk, helpdesk@example.edu, extension 4357.

## Help desk hours
The help desk is open Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00 and Saturday 9:00 to 13:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Walk-in desks are in the library on North, South and City campuses. For urgent problems outside these hours, email helpdesk@example.edu and the first agent on shift will reply.

## Wi-Fi
**How do I connect to campus Wi-Fi?** Choose the network **CampusNet** and sign in with your college email address and password. When asked about a certificate, choose Trust or Connect. CampusNet works in all buildings and hostels.

**My Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting.** Forget CampusNet on your device, restart it and connect again. If other people near you have the same problem, log a ticket with your building and floor.

**Wi-Fi for visitors.** Visitors use **CampusGuest**. They open a browser, enter their mobile number and receive a code by SMS.

## VPN
You need the VPN only to reach lab servers and the research share drive from off campus. Install **GlobalConnect VPN** from the Software Centre or portal.example.edu/vpn, then sign in with your college account and approve the MFA request. If you see an authentication error, check that your password has not changed recently and that your MFA app works.

## Passwords and MFA
**I forgot my password.** Reset it yourself at password.example.edu. You need the phone or personal email you registered in your first week. The reset email can take up to 10 minutes; check your spam folder.

**Password rules.** At least 12 characters. Passwords do not expire, but you must change yours if we suspect it has leaked. After 10 failed sign-ins your account locks for 30 minutes.

**MFA.** All students use Microsoft Authenticator. If you get a new phone, add it on the security info page (mysignins.microsoft.com) before you reset the old one. If you have already lost the old phone, visit a help desk with your student ID card.

We will never ask for your password or an MFA code.

## Printing and print credits
Print from any lab PC or send files to print@example.edu from your college email. Collect prints by tapping your student ID card on any campus printer within 24 hours.
- Black and white A4: 1 credit per page. Colour A4: 5 credits per page.
- Every student gets 50 free credits each semester.
- Buy more at portal.example.edu/print (minimum 100 credits). Credits appear within 2 hours. If they have not appeared after 24 hours, log a ticket with your payment receipt.

## Software
Windows 11 and Microsoft 365 apps are on every lab PC. Students can install Microsoft 365 on up to 5 personal devices. Lab PCs do not allow students to install software: request it with a ticket at least 5 working days before you need it. Python 3, R, MATLAB and Visual Studio Code are available in labs 1 to 4 and from the Software Centre.
