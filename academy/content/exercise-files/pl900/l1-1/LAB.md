## Steps
1. Download help-desk-requirements.md.
2. In Power Apps, open each item in the left menu once: Apps, Tables, Flows, Solutions.
3. In Power Automate, open Connectors. Search Office 365 Outlook, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Dataverse, and fill in the Premium check table.
4. Open make.powerpages.microsoft.com and browse the site templates without creating one.
5. Match each of the 10 requirements in help-desk-requirements.md to one product.
6. Write one sentence per product in the "One line per product" section.
7. Compare your answers with the answer key at the end of the file and reread the lesson for any you missed.
