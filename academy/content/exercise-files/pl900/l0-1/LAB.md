# PL-900 · 0.1 Set up your free lab

## Files
- setup-checklist.md

## Steps
1. Download setup-checklist.md and keep it open while you work.
2. Sign in to learn.microsoft.com with your personal Microsoft account and complete your profile. Tick section 1 of the checklist.
3. Sign up for the Power Apps Developer Plan with your work or school account. Tick section 2.
4. Open make.powerapps.com and pick your developer environment in the top-right environment picker. Write its name in section 3.
5. Open make.powerautomate.com and copilotstudio.microsoft.com and confirm both show the same environment (start the Copilot Studio trial if asked).
6. Open admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com, select your environment in the Environments list, and copy its name, type, region and URL into section 4.
7. Try the exam sandbox at aka.ms/examdemo and note the question types you saw in section 5.
8. Add a weekly reminder to sign in, so the environment is not disabled after 30 days unused.

## Check your work
- [ ] All four portals show the same environment name in the environment picker.
- [ ] In the admin center, your environment’s Type is Developer.
- [ ] Your environment URL ends in .dynamics.com and is written in the checklist.
- [ ] Your Microsoft Learn profile is on your personal account, not your college account.
