# PL-900 · 2.6 Environments

## Files
- environment-plan.md

## Steps
1. Download environment-plan.md.
2. In the admin center, open Environments. For your environment, fill in the type, region and Dataverse columns in Part A.
3. Create a new environment named "Help Desk Test" of type Developer.
4. When it is ready, add it to Part A.
5. In Power Apps, switch between the two environments with the environment picker and fill in Part B.
6. Complete Parts C and D: choose an environment type for each stage and explain why not to use the default environment.

## Check your work
- [ ] The admin center lists two environments of type Developer, one of them named Help Desk Test.
- [ ] Both environments have a Dataverse database.
- [ ] Help Desk Test has no Ticket, Category or Ticket Feedback table; your first environment has all three.
- [ ] In Part C, the live stage is Production and the test stage is Sandbox.
